Dana and almsgiving invitations, calm and respectful

A sanghika dana, a seven-day or three-month dana, or a pinkama at the temple. Add the family's name, an optional 'in memory of' line, the place and the time, and let relatives know on WhatsApp.

What your guests get

  • In memory of

    An optional line to remember the person the dana is offered for.

  • Temple or home

    Add the temple or the house with a Google Maps link for relatives coming from far away.

  • Sinhala first

    Write the whole invitation in Sinhala so elders read it easily.

  • Quiet designs

    Pahana, Bodhi Leaf and Dagaba White, in soft colours that suit the occasion.

  • RSVP on WhatsApp

    Know how many are coming so the dana can be prepared.

  • No music by default

    The card stays silent unless you choose otherwise.

Questions

Can I write the dana invitation fully in Sinhala?

Yes. Choose Sinhala and the card, the buttons and the RSVP all show in Sinhala.

Can I add the name of the person the dana is in memory of?

Yes. There is an optional 'In memory of' field on the almsgiving form.

Is it free?

One design and 5 personal links are free. Plus (Rs. 990 per event) covers unlimited relatives.

How much does it cost?

One design and 5 personal guest links are free for every function. Plus is Rs. 990 per event for unlimited guests, a list of who is coming and no Aradhanawa name on the card. A custom design is Rs. 4,900.

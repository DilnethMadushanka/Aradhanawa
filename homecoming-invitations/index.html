Homecoming invitations, sent in a minute

The homecoming is often a few weeks after the wedding, with a guest list of its own. Make the card, send personal links on WhatsApp and see who is coming without a single phone call.

Create your homecoming invitation, freeSee the designs

Free for 5 guests. No app to install.

What your guests get

  • Separate guest list

    Invite the groom's side for the homecoming without resending the wedding card.

  • Venue with a map pin

    Add the hotel or the family home with a Google Maps link so nobody gets lost.

  • Evening or day function

    Set the time, add a dress code or a note about dinner.

  • RSVP on WhatsApp

    Guests confirm with one tap and the reply comes to your WhatsApp.

  • Matches your wedding card

    Use the same design family as your wedding invitation, or pick Tea Country or Bentota Beach.

  • Personal for every guest

    Each link opens with the guest's name, for example 'Dear Nimal Uncle'.

Three steps, about two minutes

  1. Fill in the detailsNames, date, time and venue. Pick Sinhala, Singlish or English.
  2. Choose a designEvery function has a free design. Custom designs are Rs. 4,900.

Questions

Can I make the homecoming card separately from the wedding card?

Yes. Homecoming is its own event type with its own details and guest list.

Is the homecoming card free?

One design and 5 personal guest links are free. Plus (Rs. 990 per event) gives unlimited guests and a list of who is coming.

Can I write the card in Sinhala?

Yes. You can write in Sinhala, Singlish or English, and switch at any time before sending.

How much does it cost?

One design and 5 personal guest links are free for every function. Plus is Rs. 990 per event for unlimited guests, a list of who is coming and no Aradhanawa name on the card. A custom design is Rs. 4,900.

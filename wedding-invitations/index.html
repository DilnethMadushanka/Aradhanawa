What your guests get

  • Poruwa and nekath time

    Show the auspicious time for the poruwa ceremony, the church or temple ceremony and the reception, each in its place.

  • Both families named

    Add the groom's and the bride's parents, exactly the way a printed Sri Lankan wedding card reads.

  • Sinhala, Singlish or English

    Write the card in the language your relatives read. The guest sees Sinhala script that renders properly on any phone.

  • RSVP on WhatsApp

    Guests reply with how many are coming, and the answer lands straight in your WhatsApp.

  • Jayamangala Gatha music

    An optional soft Jayamangala Gatha plays while the card opens.

  • Designs drawn for Sri Lanka

    Poruwa Royal, Kandyan Gold, Galle Fort Sunset and more, made for Sri Lankan weddings.

Questions

Can I add the nekath time for the poruwa ceremony?

Yes. The wedding form has a separate field for the poruwa ceremony nekath time, plus the church or temple ceremony and the reception time.

Will older relatives be able to open it?

The link opens in the phone's browser straight from WhatsApp. There is nothing to install and no account needed to view it.

Can I still print cards for a few people?

Yes. Many couples send the digital card to most guests and print a small number for elders. A custom design can be prepared for print as well.

How much does it cost?

One design and 5 personal guest links are free for every function. Plus is Rs. 990 per event for unlimited guests, a list of who is coming and no Aradhanawa name on the card. A custom design is Rs. 4,900.

Questions

Can I share one link in a WhatsApp group?

Yes. You can share a general link in a group, or send personal links so each guest sees their own name.

Can I add a dress code?

Yes. The party form has a dress code field that shows on the card.

How do I see who is coming?

Guests reply on WhatsApp. With Plus (Rs. 990 per event) you also get a list of who is coming in your account.

How much does it cost?

One design and 5 personal guest links are free for every function. Plus is Rs. 990 per event for unlimited guests, a list of who is coming and no Aradhanawa name on the card. A custom design is Rs. 4,900.

## Logo prompts (Aradhanawa / ආරාධනාව)

### 1. Main logo (emblem + wordmark)
Minimal luxury logo for "Aradhanawa", a Sri Lankan digital invitation brand. A single elegant lotus flower formed from one continuous gold line, its center petal shaped like a folded invitation envelope. Below it the wordmark "Aradhanawa" in a bold high-contrast serif (Playfair Display style), deep ink green #1D2A24, with the Sinhala "ආරාධනාව" in small gold letters underneath. Champagne gold #C29A55 accents, flat vector, centered, plenty of white space, on a plain ivory #FBF8F2 background. No gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no extra text.

### 2. App icon / favicon
Square app icon for "Aradhanawa". A gold line-art lotus sitting inside a soft arch frame, like a Kandyan temple doorway, on a deep ink green #1D2A24 background. Thick clean strokes that still read at 32 px, flat vector, perfectly centered, no text, no gradients, rounded square.

### 3. Sinhala wordmark only
Typographic logo of the Sinhala word "ආරාධනාව", hand-lettered in a bold, elegant calligraphic style inspired by traditional ola-leaf script. Deep ink green #1D2A24 letters with one small gold #C29A55 lotus bud dotting the final letter. Flat vector, centered, ivory #FBF8F2 background, no other text.

Tips: generate on a plain background, then trace to SVG (Illustrator Image Trace or vectorizer.ai). AI tools often misspell Sinhala, so if letters come out wrong, generate the emblem only and type "ආරාධනාව" yourself in Noto Serif Sinhala Bold.

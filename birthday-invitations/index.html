Questions

Is it suitable for a kid's birthday party?

Yes. Most birthday cards on Aradhanawa are for children's parties, with bright designs and a countdown.

How many guests can I invite for free?

5 personal links are free. For a whole class, Plus (Rs. 990) gives unlimited guests for that event.

Can I change the time after sending?

Yes. Update the details and send the link again. Guests always see the latest version.

How much does it cost?

One design and 5 personal guest links are free for every function. Plus is Rs. 990 per event for unlimited guests, a list of who is coming and no Aradhanawa name on the card. A custom design is Rs. 4,900.
